AWS Activate is the default first stop for a startup that has settled on AWS. The headline number is real, but almost nobody gets it self-serve — the tier you land in is decided by who refers you, not by how promising your company looks.

## Why it matters

For an infrastructure-heavy company this is usually the single largest offer available anywhere. Two years of credits at the top tier can be the difference between raising a seed round on your own timeline and raising it on someone else's.

## Worth knowing

Credits expire, typically at the end of the following calendar year, and they do not roll over. Do not activate them until you have something real to run. If you are also considering Google Cloud or Azure, apply to all of them before committing architecture — you can only take each program's credits once.
