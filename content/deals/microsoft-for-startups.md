Microsoft's program is the most open of the three big clouds at the entry point and the most gated at the top. Anyone can start; the large numbers arrive only after you have proven you are a real company using real Azure services.

## Why it matters

The tiered structure means you can start immediately without a referral, which is rare. It also bundles access to OpenAI models through Azure, which matters if your compliance story needs the workload inside your own tenancy.

## Worth knowing

Do not assume you qualify for the headline figure on day one. Budget on the entry tier and treat anything above it as upside.
