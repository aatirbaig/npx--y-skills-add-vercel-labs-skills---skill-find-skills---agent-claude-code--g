A modest offer by cloud standards, but Atlas pricing bites early and the program also comes with architecture review time that is worth more than the credit itself.

## Why it matters

The technical advisory sessions are the underrated part. Getting your sharding and index strategy wrong early is expensive to unwind later.

## Worth knowing

The self-serve credit is small. If you have an investor in MongoDB's partner network, go through them instead.
