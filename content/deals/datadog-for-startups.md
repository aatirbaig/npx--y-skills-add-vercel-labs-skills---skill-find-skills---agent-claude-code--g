Observability is the classic cost that nobody budgets for until the first outage. Datadog's startup program removes that objection for the period where you can least afford the bill.

## Why it matters

Instrumenting properly while it is free means you inherit good habits. Teams that wait until they are paying tend to under-instrument permanently.

## Worth knowing

Watch the host and custom-metric counts during the free window. The bill after the program ends is a function of habits you form during it, and it surprises people.
