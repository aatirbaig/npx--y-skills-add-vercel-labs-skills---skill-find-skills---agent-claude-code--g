The largest source of non-dilutive money on this site, and the one with the longest lead time. Agencies fund technical risk, not go-to-market — the proposal has to read like research, not a pitch deck.

## Why it matters

No equity, no board seat, no liquidation preference. For deep-tech companies a Phase I and Phase II sequence can cover several years of engineering without touching the cap table.

## Worth knowing

Registration alone can take a month or more, and deadlines are hard. Treat this as a quarter of work rather than an afternoon. Proposals that read as commercial rather than technical are rejected quickly.
