Not a discount so much as an absence of the fees a traditional business account charges. For a pre-revenue company that difference is the entire point.

## Why it matters

API access and sub-accounts make runway tracking straightforward without a bookkeeping tool. Sensible defaults matter more than headline rates at this stage.

## Worth knowing

You need to be incorporated in the U.S. Non-U.S. founders generally need a U.S. entity first — see the incorporation offers in Legal & Ops.
