The discount is generous and the taper is the catch. HubSpot is betting that migrating a CRM in year three is painful enough that you will absorb the increase.

## Why it matters

For a sales-led company, having the CRM, email and reporting in one place from the start avoids a stitching project later. The first-year saving is real money at seed stage.

## Worth knowing

Model the year-two and year-three price before you commit, not the year-one price. Companies routinely sign up on the discount and discover the renewal is larger than their entire tooling budget.
