Cloudflare's program is referral-gated by design. If one of your investors is in the partner network the application is close to a formality; if none are, this offer is effectively closed to you.

## Why it matters

The credits span Workers, R2, D1 and the zero-trust products, not just the CDN. For a team building on Workers, that covers most of the infrastructure bill outright.

## Worth knowing

R2's egress economics are the real prize here — the credits are almost secondary if you are moving large volumes of data out of an object store today.
