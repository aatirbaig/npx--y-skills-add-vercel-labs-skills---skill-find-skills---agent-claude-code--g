Google's program splits into tiers, and the jump between them is steep. The AI-first track exists because Google would rather subsidise inference now than lose the workload to a competitor later.

## Why it matters

The credits are structured across two years rather than dumped in one, which suits a company whose spend ramps rather than spikes. It also comes with technical support that is genuinely useful during a migration.

## Worth knowing

Credits are scoped to a billing account. Attach the wrong one and moving them is painful. Note also that receiving anything beyond the free trial previously disqualifies you — check your old projects before applying.
