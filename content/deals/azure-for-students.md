A small credit, but it comes without a card on file — which is the actual barrier for most students rather than the money.

## Why it matters

The free service tiers persist for twelve months alongside the credit, so a low-traffic project can run for a year at genuinely zero cost.

## Worth knowing

The subscription stops rather than silently billing you when the credit runs out. That is a feature. Do not convert it to pay-as-you-go without checking what is still running.
