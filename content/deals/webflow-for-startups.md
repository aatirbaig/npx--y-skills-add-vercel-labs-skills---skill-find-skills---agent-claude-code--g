Marketing sites are the one place where engineering time is almost always the wrong resource. A covered year of Webflow buys your team out of that trap.

## Why it matters

Handing the marketing site to whoever owns marketing — rather than to the eng backlog — is usually worth more than the licence cost either way.

## Worth knowing

Site plans and workspace seats bill separately. Check which one the offer actually covers before you plan around it.
