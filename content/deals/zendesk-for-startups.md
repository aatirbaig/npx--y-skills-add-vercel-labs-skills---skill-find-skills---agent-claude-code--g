A straightforward six months free, with eligibility limits loose enough that most seed-stage companies clear them.

## Why it matters

If you are already fielding support in a shared inbox, six months is enough to migrate properly and decide whether the paid tier earns its place.

## Worth knowing

Migrating out later means exporting ticket history, which is tedious. Decide during the free window rather than after it.
