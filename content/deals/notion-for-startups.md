One of the easiest offers to actually redeem — a code, a page, done — which is rare among programs of this size.

## Why it matters

Notion's per-seat pricing gets uncomfortable fast as you hire. A covered year lets you standardise on it before the cost of switching is high.

## Worth knowing

The credit is capped in dollars, not in time. A team that grows quickly will burn through it well before the twelve months are up.
