Unglamorous and easy to approve. If your team already lives in IntelliJ or PyCharm, this is a straightforward halving of a bill you were going to pay anyway.

## Why it matters

The All Products Pack covers every JetBrains IDE, so a polyglot team stops arguing about which licences to buy.

## Worth knowing

The discount steps down after the first year. Diarise the renewal so the increase is not a surprise.
