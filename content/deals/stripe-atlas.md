Listed here because it is the unblocking step for most non-U.S. founders, not because it is free — Atlas charges a flat fee and is upfront about it.

## Why it matters

Doing incorporation, EIN, bank account and founder equity paperwork as one flow removes weeks of sequencing, especially the EIN, which is the usual bottleneck for non-residents.

## Worth knowing

This is a paid service. It is cheaper than a law firm for a standard Delaware C-corp and worse than one for anything unusual. If your cap table is not vanilla, get a lawyer.
