Hatch is the pragmatic choice for teams who want predictable pricing and do not need the sprawl of a hyperscaler. The credits go further because the underlying prices are lower.

## Why it matters

A dollar of DigitalOcean credit buys materially more compute than a dollar of AWS credit. If your workload is straightforward — containers, managed Postgres, object storage — the effective runway extension is larger than the headline suggests.

## Worth knowing

Fewer managed services means more of the operational burden stays with you. That is a fair trade at four engineers and a bad one at forty.
