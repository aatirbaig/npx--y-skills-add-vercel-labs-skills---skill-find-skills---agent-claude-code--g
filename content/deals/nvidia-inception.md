Inception is free to join and unusually broad. It is less a discount than a relationship: hardware pricing, credits with cloud partners, and introductions that are hard to get any other way.

## Why it matters

The GPU discounts matter most to teams buying their own hardware. For everyone else the value is in the partner credit offers and the co-marketing, which are genuinely useful at seed stage.

## Worth knowing

Benefits are tiered and the good ones sit above the entry level. Treat acceptance as the start of the conversation rather than the payoff.
