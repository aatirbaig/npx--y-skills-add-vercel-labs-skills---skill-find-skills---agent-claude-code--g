Segment's pricing scales with tracked users, which is exactly the axis a growing startup moves along fastest. A year of credits buys you the period where that curve is steepest.

## Why it matters

Getting the event schema right early is one of the few data decisions that is genuinely hard to redo. Doing it while the bill is covered removes the temptation to under-instrument.

## Worth knowing

Plan for the post-credit bill before you route every event through it. Teams that instrument everything during the free year get a shock at renewal.
